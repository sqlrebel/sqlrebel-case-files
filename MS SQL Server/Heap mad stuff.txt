Heaps and Page compression are not friends.
I found out this to my cost one day, if you have page compression enabled on a heap and data gets inserted into that heap, any new allocated pages are not compressed.
How crazy is that.
Newly allocated pages will only get compressed for the following two reasons:
  1. The heap is rebuilt
  2. The data is bulk imported into the heap.

This is mad, like what's the logic behind this.
